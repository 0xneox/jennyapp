Privacy Policy — JennyDoko

Last updated: October 10, 2026

The short version

JennyDoko is a puzzle game supported by ads that can be played fully offline. We never ask for your name, email, or any account. Your progress, settings, and stats stay on your device unless you choose to join the optional global leaderboard, which shares only a made-up player name, a cartoon avatar, and your game points (see "Optional global leaderboard"). We never sell data. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which processes limited device data to deliver and measure ads (see "Advertising" below).

Optional global leaderboard

The leaderboard is off until you tap "Join the Leaderboard". If you join, the app sends the following to our leaderboard server (hosted by Supabase):

  • A random ID created on your device (stored only as a one-way hash, not linked to your device, advertising ID, or any account)
  • Your player name, chosen only from a fixed list of friendly words plus a number — you can't type free text
  • Your chosen cartoon avatar
  • Your best score on each level and the dates you completed the Daily Garden, used to calculate your points

Other players can see your player name, avatar, rank, and points. As with any internet connection, the server receives your IP address to answer requests; we don't store it with your leaderboard entry.

You can leave at any time from the Leaderboard → your avatar → "Leave leaderboard & delete my scores". This permanently deletes your leaderboard entry and scores from the server.

What is stored on your device

The game saves the following locally on your device (not sent to us or anyone else, except the leaderboard data listed above if you join):

  • Level progress and unlocked levels
  • Game mode, sound, and music settings
  • Daily challenge streaks and stats
  • Story and tutorial "seen" flags
  • Your ad consent choice (where consent is required)
  • Your leaderboard player name, avatar, and random ID

This data lives in your browser's local storage (web) or the app's private storage (Android/iOS).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

JennyDoko shows ads provided by Google AdMob — banners, occasional interstitials between levels, and rewarded ads you can choose to watch for extra hints. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may process your device's advertising ID, IP address, and ad interaction data. Ad content is capped at a family-appropriate content rating.

  • Where required by law (EEA, UK, Switzerland), a consent form lets you choose between personalized and non-personalized ads.
  • You can review or change your choice any time from Settings → Privacy & Ads.
  • No rewarded-ad reward is granted unless the ad finishes playing.

See how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

What we do NOT collect

  • No accounts, sign-ups, names, or email addresses
  • No free-text input of any kind
  • No analytics or tracking by us
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, or files
  • No crash reporting sent to third parties

(Our ad partner Google processes its own data under Google's policies — see "Advertising".)

Children's privacy

JennyDoko does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and the game can be played fully offline. The optional leaderboard was designed for kids: names can only be built from a fixed list of friendly words, avatars are cartoons, and nothing identifies the player in real life. Ads shown are limited to a family-appropriate content rating.

Data Safety (Play Store)

For Play Console Data Safety: the app shares the device advertising ID with Google AdMob for advertising purposes. If the player joins the optional leaderboard, the app collects in-game app activity (a made-up player name, avatar, scores, and daily completion dates) and a random app-generated ID, used only to run the leaderboard. This data is encrypted in transit, is not shared with third parties or sold, and can be deleted in-app at any time.

Deleting your data

To delete your leaderboard data, open Leaderboard → your avatar → "Leave leaderboard & delete my scores". Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data (if you uninstall without leaving the leaderboard first, email us your player name and we will remove that entry). On the web version, clearing your browser's site data removes local data.

Changes to this policy

If JennyDoko adds new online features, this policy will be updated before those features ship, and any new data handling will be described here.
